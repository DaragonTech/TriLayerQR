TriLayerQR Simple

Everyday QR codes that hold more. Your text is compressed and, when it makes the code clearly smaller, split across the colors of the code: green carries one half, red and blue carry the other. Short texts such as links stay plain black and white, readable by any phone.

Create

Type something to create a code.

Color codes are read by this page (camera or image). Black-and-white codes are ordinary QR codes that any scanner reads.

Read

Scan a code with the camera, or open an image of one.
